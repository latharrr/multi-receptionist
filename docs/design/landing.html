You set it up yourself, from what you already have.

No one hand-writes prompts for you. Each step is saved, so you can stop and come back.

  1. 1

    Add what you already have

    Website pages you choose, a text or Markdown file, or pasted FAQs. Only the pages you pick are read.

  2. 2

    Review every fact

    Each fact shows where it came from. Conflicts and gaps are flagged, and it asks about what’s missing one question at a time.

  3. 3

    Choose your AI provider and WhatsApp route

    Claude by default, or your own Groq, NVIDIA or compatible key. Then decide how WhatsApp connects.

  4. 4

    Test before a customer sees it

    Run sample chats against the exact version you are about to publish. Mock tests are labelled as mock.

  5. 5

    Switch it on yourself

    One summary shows the number, route, facts, model and spending limit. You press Activate. Pause stops new automated replies; a message already submitted may still arrive.

Two ways to connect WhatsApp. Know the difference before you choose.

Official Cloud API In development

What it is

Meta’s own business API for WhatsApp.

How it connects

Through Meta’s business sign-up. Messages reach us from Meta’s servers.

Before you start

Business and number checks with Meta. Not every number is eligible.

What to expect

Meta’s messaging rules and charges apply, and setup takes longer.

Main risk

Some replies can be blocked by Meta’s rules. The dashboard tells you why.

Unofficial QR link In development

What it is

Links your existing number the way WhatsApp Web does, through an unofficial library.

How it connects

As a linked device, by scanning a QR code. This is not an official integration.

Before you start

Your phone, to scan a QR code, and your agreement to the risks below.

What to expect

Quick to link. The session can drop and need linking again.

Main risk

WhatsApp can restrict or disconnect the account.

A receptionist, not a salesperson or a cashier.

It will

  • Answer from the facts you have published, in one or two short lines.
  • Take down the enquiry details you choose to collect.
  • Hand uncertain conversations to your staff.
  • Stop starting new automated replies when you pause it or take over a chat. A message already submitted may still arrive.

It won’t

  • Take payments or run a checkout.
  • Confirm appointments on its own.
  • Send cold or bulk messages.
  • Handle voice notes, images or other media.
  • Treat what a customer says as your business policy.

Pilot places are by enquiry.

Pricing and trial terms aren’t set yet. Tell us what your front desk handles and we’ll reply when a pilot place opens.
